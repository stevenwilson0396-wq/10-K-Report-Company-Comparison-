10-K Company Comparison

Compare two to five public companies side by side: financial statements, ratios, stock performance and the tone of management's discussion, all from SEC 10-K filings.

    Search any SEC company, or add one from a file

    Live search. Type in the Company A or Company B box to search every company registered with the SEC. The page then fetches that company's filings and prices and works out every ratio on the spot.     MD&A tone is not available for companies loaded live, because it needs each filing's full text. To include it, add the company with python pipeline/add_company.py TICKER and commit the files in data/ (or use Actions → Add company on GitHub).

    Load a company file: pick a company JSON made by that script and it is added for this visit only.

    Figures come from SEC EDGAR XBRL filings and Yahoo Finance closing prices, processed by the scripts in pipeline/. Fiscal years use each company's own year-end, so the same label can cover different months. Not investment advice. Check important numbers against the filings.
